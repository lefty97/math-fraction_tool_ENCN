🔎 Maths Concept Diagnostic

A teacher-guided assessment for a strong Year 4 student preparing for Year 5. The goal is to investigate mathematical understanding—not speed, memorized rules, or the number of calculations completed.

1 · PredictChoose or write an answer before calculating when possible.
2 · ExplainSay WHY. Use words, drawings, examples or a number line.
3 · DiscussThe teacher reveals the guide and records understanding.

Core: 10 questions (about 30–40 minutes). Extension: 5 questions if time allows (a further 15–25 minutes). No timer, no immediate right/wrong messages, and no automatic grading.

Teacher rubric: 2 = independent, correct reasoning; 1 = partial reasoning or correct after prompting; 0 = not yet demonstrated. A correct guess without a sound explanation is not a 2. Opening a hint is recorded. Responses are saved only in this browser; export your report to keep a copy.
