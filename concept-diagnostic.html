🔎 Maths Concept Diagnostic

Four teacher-guided mini-assessments for a strong Year 4 student preparing for Year 5. Each section takes about 10–15 minutes and investigates understanding, not speed or memorized rules.

1 · PredictChoose or write an answer before calculating when possible.
2 · ExplainSay WHY. Use words, drawings, examples or a number line.
3 · DiscussThe teacher reveals the guide and records understanding.

Four sections · 10–15 minutes each. Each section has four core investigations and one optional challenge. You may do one section per lesson. The 12-minute timer is optional and never submits or locks the assessment.

Teacher rubric: 2 = independent, correct reasoning; 1 = partial reasoning or correct after prompting; 0 = not yet demonstrated. Record accuracy, explanations, models and transfer separately. Opening a hint is recorded. Responses are saved only in this browser; export your report to keep a copy.
